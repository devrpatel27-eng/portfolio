About Us

A friendly, well-stocked stop for the Littleton community

Littleton Convenience is a locally run liquor and convenience store right on Jennifer St. Whether you're grabbing a six-pack on the way home, picking up a bottle for dinner, playing the lottery, or just need a quick grocery run, we've got you covered.

We keep a deep selection of beer, wine, and spirits — from everyday favorites to top-shelf picks — alongside cigarettes, snacks, drinks, and lottery. Stop in, say hi, and find what you need fast.

  • Wide selection of craft & domestic beer
  • Wine and spirits for every occasion
  • MA Lottery & scratch tickets
  • Cigarettes & tobacco
  • Grocery & snack essentials
  • ATM, EBT accepted, and packaged ice
